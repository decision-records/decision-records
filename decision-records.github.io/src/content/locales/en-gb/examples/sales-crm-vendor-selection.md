# Select a CRM platform

* Status: accepted
* Date: 2026-02-02
* Decision-maker(s): VP Sales
* Owner: Sales Operations Manager

## Context

Our current CRM was implemented seven years ago and no longer supports the automation our sales process needs — reps spend an estimated 5 hours per week on manual data entry that competing tools automate. The contract renews in 90 days, giving us a natural switch point. Sales Operations, Marketing, and Finance all depend on this system and need to agree on the replacement.

## Options considered

* **Renew current CRM, invest in custom automation add-ons.** Avoids migration risk and retraining, but the vendor has confirmed no roadmap improvements to the underlying data model our automation gaps stem from — this treats the symptom, not the cause.
* **Switch to Platform A.** Strong marketing-automation integration (a current gap) and the best reference-call feedback on data migration support; standard implementation timeline is 8–10 weeks.
* **Switch to Platform B.** Lowest sticker price, but two reference calls independently raised concerns about support responsiveness during migration, and it lacks native integration with our finance system, which would require a custom connector.

## Decision

We will switch to Platform A, with migration beginning immediately after contract termination notice is filed with the current vendor.

## Consequences

* Sales Operations will lead an 8–10 week migration, with a parallel-run period before full cutover.
* All reps require training before cutover; Sales Operations will schedule this in the two weeks prior.
* Current CRM contract must be terminated with required notice before the new term would otherwise auto-renew.
* We accept short-term productivity dip during migration and training in exchange for the automation gains projected to save each rep roughly 5 hours per week starting the following quarter.
* Marketing and Finance integrations will be validated in a staging environment before cutover, given Platform A's stronger reference feedback on migration support.

## Related

* Sales Operations will document the final migration runbook as a follow-on artifact once complete.
