# Move to usage-based pricing

* Status: accepted
* Date: 2026-04-05
* Decision-maker(s): VP Product, with CFO sign-off
* Owner: Head of Pricing & Packaging

## Context

Our current flat per-seat pricing under-monetizes our highest-usage customers and over-charges our lightest users, who are churning at 2x the rate of high-usage accounts. Three of our top five competitors have shifted to usage-based or hybrid pricing in the last 18 months, and prospects increasingly ask why we haven't.

## Options considered

* **Keep flat per-seat pricing.** No migration risk or customer disruption, but doesn't address the churn pattern among light users or the under-monetization of heavy users; competitively, we're increasingly the outlier.
* **Move fully to usage-based pricing.** Best aligns price with value delivered, but finance modeling shows meaningful revenue volatility quarter to quarter, and it's the most disruptive change for existing customers to absorb at once.
* **Move to a hybrid model: a lower seat-based base fee plus usage-based overage.** Smooths revenue volatility relative to full usage-based pricing while still fixing the light-user overcharge and heavy-user under-monetization; existing customers can be migrated with a grandfathering period.

## Decision

We will move to a hybrid pricing model — reduced base seat fee plus usage-based overage — with new customers on the new model immediately and existing customers grandfathered for two quarters before migration.

## Consequences

* Existing customers must be individually modeled to confirm no one faces a net price increase during the grandfathering period without advance notice.
* Sales compensation plans need to be updated to reflect the new pricing structure before the new model goes live for new deals.
* Finance will revise revenue forecasting models to account for usage-based volatility.
* Customer Success will proactively communicate the change to at-risk light-usage accounts, since the new model should reduce (not increase) their bill.
* We accept short-term forecasting complexity in exchange for better price-to-value alignment and a stronger competitive position.
