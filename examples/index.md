Notes on this index:

* Each example is one self-contained file — the same convention recommended for your own decision log.
* Status, date, and decision-maker are filled in on every example, modeling what a real record should always include.
* Two templates are represented deliberately: comprehensive for higher-stakes, multi-stakeholder calls; lightweight for everyday, scoped decisions. See [template/index.md](../template/index.md#choosing-which-template-to-use) for how to choose.
* A technology example is included even though the target audience is business, technology, and strategy leaders together — architecture decisions are one instance of this same general pattern, not a separate discipline.
