# Adopt contact relationship management

* Status: accepted
* Date: 2026-10-12
* Decision-maker(s): Chief Revenue Officer
* Owner: Head of Revenue Operations

## Context

Our customer and partner contacts are held in individual inboxes, personal spreadsheets, and three different tools. When an account manager leaves, their relationships leave too, and we have twice contacted the same person on the same day from different teams. We also cannot show where in our organisation a person's consent to be contacted is recorded. We need one shared way to manage contacts and our relationships with them, covering customers, partners, and suppliers, not only sales prospects.

## Options considered

* **Keep the current mix of tools and spreadsheets.** No cost or change effort, but the problems of lost relationships, duplicate contact, and unclear consent continue.
* **Adopt one shared contact relationship management system for the whole company.** One record per person and organisation, a shared history of interactions, and recorded consent. Fixes the problems, but it needs data migration, rules for who may see what, and a change in habits from account managers.
* **Add contact management to the existing sales CRM only.** Quickest, and we know the tool, but it would leave partners and suppliers out and keep contacts held by non-sales teams separate.

## Decision

We will adopt one shared contact relationship management system for the whole company, with one record per person and organisation, a shared history of interactions, and the person's consent and contact preferences recorded on the record. We will migrate in stages, starting with the sales and customer support teams, and retire the other tools once their data has moved.

## Consequences

* The Head of Revenue Operations will lead the selection, the data migration, and the rules for who may see and change which records.
* Contact details and consent will be recorded in one place, and any request from a person to see, correct, or delete their data will be handled from that record.
* Account managers are expected to log their interactions in the system; we will check this in the first quarter and adjust the process, not just the reminders, if it is not happening.
* We will clean and merge duplicate contacts during migration, which will take staff time that we accept.
* We give up the freedom to use personal spreadsheets, in exchange for relationships that stay with the company and are easier to protect.
