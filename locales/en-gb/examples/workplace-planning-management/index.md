# Adopt workplace planning and management

* Status: accepted
* Date: 2026-10-08
* Decision-maker(s): Chief Operating Officer
* Owner: Head of Workplace

## Context

After we chose team-set anchor days for hybrid working, office use became much harder to predict: badge data shows 31% average use on a Monday and 74% on a Wednesday, and teams that share a floor have found no room to meet on the same day. Space, desks, meeting rooms, cleaning, and security are managed by three separate groups with separate data. We need a way to plan and manage the workplace as one system, using evidence of how it is used, so that the space fits the work and we spend our property budget well.

## Options considered

* **Keep managing space by group.** No change in effort, but decisions about space, bookings, and services continue to disagree with one another, and we cannot see the whole picture.
* **Buy an integrated workplace management platform and let it assign desks.** Complete data and automation, but it is costly, and automatic assignment ignores what teams need to be together.
* **Plan the workplace from usage data, with team bookings.** One owner for space, bookings, and services; badge, booking, and sensor-free data (no tracking of individuals); a quarterly plan of space by day; and bookable team zones. Matches space to demand and keeps teams together, but it needs one owner, a decision on what data to collect, and support from the teams.

## Decision

We will plan and manage the workplace as one system under a Head of Workplace, using aggregated usage data from badge and booking systems (never individual tracking), a quarterly plan of space by day, and bookable team zones so that teams meet on the same days. We will review the amount of space we lease at the next break point using the first year of data.

## Consequences

* The Head of Workplace will own space, bookings, cleaning, and security services, and will publish the quarterly plan.
* We will report use only in aggregate, by floor and by day, and will not report on individuals; we will explain this to staff before collecting the data.
* Teams will book their zones for the days of their written agreements, so that anchor days actually bring people together.
* Desks and rooms will be fitted to the demand we measure, which may mean fewer fixed desks and more meeting and collaboration space.
* Adjustments for individuals follow [Set a process for workplace reasonable adjustments](../workplace-reasonable-adjustments/) and are not affected by desk booking rules.
